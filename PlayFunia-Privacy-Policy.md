## 3. SMS Text Messaging Program

### How SMS Works at PlayFunia

When you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages from PlayFunia, you are enrolling in our SMS messaging program. Here's what you need to know:

### What Messages You'll Receive
- **Booking Confirmations:** Confirmation of your reservation details
- **Reminders:** Notifications before your scheduled visit/party
- **Updates:** Important information about your booking or facility changes
- **Promotional Messages:** Occasional special offers, discounts, and news (frequency may vary, typically 2-4 messages per month)

### Message Frequency
Message frequency varies depending on your bookings and promotions. You may receive:
- Transactional messages (confirmations, reminders) as needed for your bookings
- Marketing messages approximately 2-4 times per month

### SMS Costs
Standard message and data rates apply based on your mobile carrier's plan. PlayFunia does not charge for messages, but your carrier may charge you for receiving text messages.

### How to Opt In
You can opt in to receive SMS messages by:
- Checking the SMS consent box when booking online at https://playfunia.com
- Providing your phone number and verbal consent when booking in person at our facility
- Texting "START" to our SMS number (if we offer keyword opt-in)

When you opt in, you explicitly agree to receive automated text messages from PlayFunia about bookings, reminders, and promotional offers.

### How to Opt Out (Unsubscribe)
You can stop receiving text messages at any time by:
- Replying "STOP," "UNSUBSCRIBE," "END," "QUIT," or "CANCEL" to any message from PlayFunia
- Contacting us directly at info@playfunia.com or calling us during business hours

Once you opt out, you will receive one final confirmation message, and then you will not receive any further text messages from PlayFunia unless you opt in again. Please note that opting out of SMS does not cancel any existing bookings or affect your ability to use our services.

### How to Get Help
If you have questions about our SMS program or need assistance, you can:
- Reply "HELP" or "INFO" to any message from PlayFunia
- Email us at info@playfunia.com
- Call us at [Your Phone Number]

### SMS Consent and TCPA Compliance
By opting in to our SMS program, you provide your prior express written consent to receive automated text messages from PlayFunia to the mobile phone number you provided. This consent is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services from PlayFunia.

We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), CTIA Messaging Principles and Best Practices, and mobile carrier requirements for A2P 10DLC messaging.

### SMS Data Collection and Storage
When you opt in to SMS, we collect and store:
- Your mobile phone number
- The date and time you opted in
- The method you used to opt in (web form, in-person, keyword)
- The page or location where you opted in
- Your IP address at the time of opt-in (for web opt-ins)

We retain this consent information as proof of your agreement to receive messages and for compliance purposes.

### Supported Mobile Carriers
Our SMS program is supported by all major U.S. mobile carriers including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and others. If you experience issues receiving messages, please contact your mobile carrier or reach out to us for assistance.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. We use cookies to:
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Understand how you use our website
- Improve website performance and user experience
- Provide targeted advertising

### Types of Cookies We Use
- **Essential Cookies:** Required for the website to function (e.g., shopping cart, login sessions)
- **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand website traffic and user behavior (e.g., Google Analytics)
- **Marketing Cookies:** Track your activity to show relevant advertisements
- **Preference Cookies:** Remember your choices and settings

### Managing Cookies
You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- Block all cookies
- Accept only certain cookies
- Delete cookies after your session

Note that disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of our website.

## 6. Children's Privacy

PlayFunia provides services primarily for children aged 1-13, but we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

When you book a party or visit for your child, we collect information from you (the parent or guardian), not directly from the child. This may include:
- Parent/guardian contact information
- Child's first name and age (for party planning purposes)
- Number of children attending
- Special requests or dietary restrictions

### COPPA Compliance
We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not:
- Collect personal information directly from children under 13 through our website
- Require children to provide personal information to participate in activities
- Share children's information with third parties for marketing purposes

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us immediately at info@playfunia.com, and we will delete it promptly.
